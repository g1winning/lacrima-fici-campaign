Image plan

Twenty-eight frames were rendered locally for this package with FLUX.1 schnell, in one visual world: deep aubergine, cream linen, antique gold, sage green, soft window light, no text in the image. They are concept renders, good enough to brief a photographer and to show the executive team what each tactic looks like. Where a frame is marked "re-shoot", a real photograph at the orchard or in a kitchen would replace it before publication; renders of hands, faces and the actual bottle are the ones a photographer should take.

Two cautions

The renders show a generic flask bottle with a blank cream label. Any image a buyer or a journalist sees must carry the real Lacrima Fici label, so the bottle frames are placeholders for the product photography. And no image may show a health claim, a sugar claim or a glycaemic claim in any form.
