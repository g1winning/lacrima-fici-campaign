Trade campaign copy

Everything a retail buyer will read, from the box on their desk to the LinkedIn post that reaches them the week before. All pricing below assumes the brief: $7.50 wholesale per 200 mL bottle in cases of 12, $12.00 recommended retail, deliveries from mid February 2027. Where the brief's earlier figure of $12.50 is preferred, the margin line changes to 40 per cent and nothing else moves.

1. Sell sheet (one page, A4, front and back)

Front.

Lacrima Fici. Tear of the Fig. The pure syrup grown in Victoria.

100 per cent Australian Black fig syrup. One ingredient. No preservatives, no added sugar, no thickeners. Pressed by the Romeo family at Shepparton, where they have grown figs since 1944.

Australia imported 3,731 tonnes of maple syrup in 2025, a fifth more than the year before, and sells most of it at $6.00 a bottle as a loss leader. Lacrima Fici is the Australian line that earns the shelf its margin: a premium pure syrup at $12.00 that returns $4.50 a bottle against about $1.70 on an own-brand maple, with a cleaner, less dominating sweetness that works at breakfast, on cheese, in a glaze and in a glass.

200 mL flask bottle. Recommended retail $12.00. Wholesale $7.50 per bottle, $90.00 per case of 12. Retailer gross margin 37.5 per cent. Shelf life 12 months ambient, refrigerate after opening. Deliveries from 15 February 2027, the start of the Victorian fig harvest.

Back.

Uses. Pancakes, waffles and porridge in place of maple. Over yoghurt, ricotta and gelato. With blue cheese, pecorino and prosciutto. A tablespoon to finish a stir-fry or glaze a pork roast. Half a measure in a whisky sour or an old fashioned.

Facings. Shelve beside pure maple syrup in table syrups; a second facing in the deli or cheese bay sells the cheese use.

Logistics. Case of 12, outer dimensions and weight on the specification page. GS1 barcode on bottle and case. Pallet configuration and lead times on request. Minimum order one case. Freight at cost on orders under four cases; a Melbourne delivery run fortnightly through the season.

Support. Shelf talkers and a recipe card for every ranging store. In-store tastings in Victorian stores from the week of 15 February 2027. Consumer launch across Instagram, Facebook and the food press from the same week, timed to the harvest. Feature coverage pitched to Good Food, delicious., Gourmet Traveller and Broadsheet.

Contact. Santo Romeo, Romeo Farm, 215 Central Avenue, Shepparton VIC 3631. 0477 815 638.

6. Trade media release

For release Monday 16 November 2026.

Shepparton fig growers launch Australia's pure table syrup to retail.

The Romeo family, who have grown Black figs on the same Shepparton orchard since 1944, will supply their pure fig syrup, Lacrima Fici, to Australian retailers from February 2027.

The syrup is made from a single ingredient. Ripe figs are crushed and warmed until they thicken, then bottled with no sugar, preservative or thickener added. The result is a dark amber table syrup with a cleaner, less dominating sweetness than maple, used at breakfast, on cheese, in glazes and in cocktails.

Santo Romeo said the pure syrup shelf in Australian supermarkets was entirely imported. "Maple imports grew by a fifth last year. We can give the shelf an Australian pure syrup beside them. The figs are grown here, pressed here and bottled here."

Lacrima Fici is supplied in a 200 mL flask bottle at a recommended retail price of $12.00 and a wholesale price of $7.50 in cases of 12. First deliveries coincide with the Victorian fig harvest in mid February 2027. In-store tastings, shelf material and a consumer campaign in the food press begin the same week.

Lacrima Fici is Latin for "tear of the fig", after the bead of syrup a fully ripe fig weeps on the tree.

Trade enquiries: Santo Romeo, Romeo Farm, 215 Central Avenue, Shepparton VIC 3631, 0477 815 638.
