The venues, grocers and distributors a buyer's box should reach.

Market research on the supply chain between the orchard and the glass: about 190 named decision-makers at roughly 110 Victorian businesses, with the cocktail bars, Peninsula hotels, gelaterias and specialty distributors picked out.

The research tool holds companies with a public profile, filtered here to Victoria across restaurants, bars, hotels, hospitality groups, food retail, grocers, wineries, distilleries and food wholesalers, then narrowed by the words Shepparton, Goulburn Valley, Carlton, Lygon Street, Mornington Peninsula, Sorrento, Red Hill, Flinders, Surf Coast, Torquay, Lorne, Anglesea, cocktail, gelato, ice cream and cheese on their websites. From the 2,088 businesses matching, the 300 largest were sampled, and two lists of people were drawn from them: owners, founders and directors; and head chefs, beverage, bar, purchasing, venue and general managers. Every person on the list has a work email on file, verified at the time of export; mobile numbers were found for six.

Two limits are worth knowing. The tool is strongest on groups and companies large enough to have a staff page, so the single-site Shepparton cafe or the Torquay deli is largely absent; those targets come from local knowledge, the Shepparton News and a drive down the main street, not from a database. And the seniority search reads "director" literally, so it returned HR, data and sales directors at the larger companies alongside owners; those rows are marked in the private list and left out of the tables below. The named people, their emails and the full export sit in a private file for Santo and the agency, not on this page.

The venues worth a box first are the cocktail-led rooms (Gimlet, the Speakeasy Group's bars), the Peninsula's hotels and winery restaurants (Jackalope, The Continental Sorrento, Montalto, Polperro, Green Olive at Red Hill, Lindenderry through Lancemore), the gelato and frozen yoghurt groups (Piccolina, Yo-Chi, YOMG) for the topping use, the Carlton institutions (Brunetti Classico, Seven Seeds) for breakfast, and Scicluna's at Mount Eliza as a Peninsula shelf. The distributors are the multiplier: City Larder, Gippsland Cheese, 5Ways Foodservice and My Local Foodie each supply dozens of venues, and two of them have a purchasing or procurement head on the list.
